The **services sector** is **the largest and fastest-growing pillar of the Indian economy**, driving a major share of national output, employment, and international trade. 

**Key Growth and Economic Metrics**

- **GDP and GVA Contribution:** The services sector accounts for approximately **54% to 55% of India’s GDP** and around **56.4% of Gross Value Added (GVA)**. 
- **Growth Rate:** The sector expanded by **9.1%**, significantly outpacing agricultural and industrial segments. [[1](https://www.drishtiias.com/daily-updates/daily-news-editorials/service-sector-as-growth-engine)]
- **Global Trade and Exports:** India has grown into a major global hub for commercial services. Services exports reached a record **US$ 387.5 billion**, with India’s share of global commercial services exports rising to **4.3%**.
