## 1. What is GST?

Goods and Services Tax (GST) is an indirect tax system introduced in India on 1 July 2017.

It replaced several indirect taxes and brought many goods and services under a common tax framework.

GST is collected through different components:

- **CGST:** Central Goods and Services Tax
- **SGST:** State Goods and Services Tax
- **IGST:** Integrated Goods and Services Tax
- **Cess:** Applicable to specified goods and services

## 2. October 2026 GST Collection: Key Highlights

- **Gross GST collection:** Approximately ₹2.31 lakh crore.
- **Year-on-year growth:** 22.9%.
- **Net GST revenue:** Approximately ₹2.03 lakh crore.
- October's collection was the second-highest monthly gross collection reported in 2026, after April's ₹2.43 lakh crore.

## 3. Why is Higher GST Collection Important?

Higher GST collection can indicate several economic developments:

1. **Consumption:** Increased purchases of goods and services can contribute to higher tax collections.
2. **Business activity:** Economic transactions and business operations contribute to GST revenue.
3. **Government revenue:** Tax revenue supports public expenditure and development programmes.
4. **Tax compliance:** Better reporting and compliance can also improve collections.

However, GST growth alone does not prove that every sector of the economy is performing equally well.
