# My Journey: A Dream, A Vision & The Courage to Never Give Up

**By Porush Sharma**

## Every Dream Has a Beginning

Hello everyone!

I am Porush Sharma, a dreamer from Bihar and an aspiring civil servant with a vision to create a meaningful difference in society.

My journey is not just about preparing for an examination. It is about discovering myself, learning through experiences, building discipline, and becoming a better version of who I was yesterday.

Coming from Bihar, I have always believed that our background does not decide our future. Our determination, hard work, and willingness to learn shape the path ahead.

## The Power of Self-Study

I believe in the power of self-study.

My learning journey has taught me that you don't always need perfect circumstances to begin something meaningful. What you need is consistency, curiosity, patience, and the courage to keep moving forward.

There are days when things feel difficult. There are moments of doubt, uncertainty, and challenges. But every difficult day teaches us something valuable.

I have learned that success is not built in a single day. It is built through small efforts repeated every single day.

## My Vision

As an IAS aspirant, my dream is bigger than just clearing an examination.

I want to understand society, learn about the challenges people face, and contribute towards a better and more inclusive future.

I know that the journey is long, but I am ready to learn, grow, and give my best.

## Why I Started This Blog

I started this platform to share knowledge, ideas, experiences, and thoughts that can make a positive difference.

Here, I want to explore different subjects, including:

- Education and self-improvement
- UPSC preparation and learning
- Science and technology
- Current affairs and society
- Motivation and personal development
- Knowledge, ideas, and life lessons

My goal is to make learning meaningful, accessible, and inspiring.

## A Message to Every Dreamer

If you come from a small town, have limited resources, or are preparing for a big dream through self-study, remember one thing:

**Your starting point does not define your destination.**

Never underestimate the power of your efforts. Don't compare your journey with someone else's. Focus on your growth, stay disciplined, and keep believing in your potential.

You may not control every situation in life, but you can always control your dedication and attitude.

I am still learning. I am still growing. My journey is still unfolding.

And this is just the beginning.

**Dream big. Stay humble. Keep learning. Never give up.**

— Porush Sharma
